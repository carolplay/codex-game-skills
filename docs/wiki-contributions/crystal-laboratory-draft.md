## 建议补充的英文段落

Ordinary Refinement and Super Refinement use different materials. Ordinary Refinement converts meat, wood, coal, and iron into Fire Crystals; the displayed resource cost increases between refinements. A daily-discounted Super Refinement still consumes Fire Crystals and should not be described as a free reward.

Check the current price and daily-special indicator before selecting the single-refinement button. In the observed client, the first discounted Super Refinement cost10 Fire Crystals; afterward its displayed price returned to20. The weekly Super Refinement counter records uses already made, not remaining free attempts. Check the current client description and progression stage instead of assuming every account has the same allowance or output.
